# AmneziaWG Config Generator 3.2.0

## An own address catalogue for presets, a new service catalogue, the Endpoint never inside the routes

Prepared on 2026-11-01 after 3.1.9 (`cebc09e`). Full-tunnel configs do not change: without presets, or with `routeMode=full`, the `.conf`, `vpn://` and the response fields are those of 3.1.9 (checked against `v3.1.9`, see the source audit).

## What changed for users

**Presets take their addresses from the site's own catalogue.** Until 3.1.9 every preset asked iplist.opencck.org at request time with its domains. That service answers only for the names of its "portals", and when a whole set came back empty the generator put in a general list of 71 antifilter networks: in the measurement of 2026-11-01, 24 of the 49 presets of 3.1.9 got that list instead of addresses of their own. From 3.2.0 a catalogue preset takes its networks from `src/server/ipCatalog/snapshot.json`, which `npm run ipcatalog:build` builds in advance from:

- official lists: Telegram `cidr.txt`, Cloudflare `ips-v4`/`ips-v6`, GitHub `meta` without `actions`, Zoom `Zoom.txt`;
- announcements of the services' AS (ipverse, CC0; iptoasn, PDDL, when ipverse cannot be read); for Google and YouTube the announcements of Google's AS minus the Google Cloud ranges of `cloud.json`;
- the project's own DNS-over-HTTPS resolution (Google DoH) of the preset's domains and of the domains of opencck portals from the rekryt/iplist configs (MIT), asked with the client subnets of Moscow (`95.165.0.0/24`), Saint Petersburg (`178.70.0.0/24`) and Germany (`85.214.0.0/24`); a resolved address stays 30 days after it was last seen.

"Routes" is the number of IPv4 lines the preset alone gives in `AllowedIPs` after the WARP endpoint cut and the exact merge. "Exact" means official lists and AS announcements; "approximate" means the own resolution is part of it; "wide" means more than 300 routes (or a recipe of more than 600 domains, which is not resolved at all); a transitional preset is always marked "wide", whatever its size, since its addresses come from the live lists. Eleven of these presets — all but Roblox, Control4 and the new ones — got one and the same list of 71 antifilter networks in the 3.1.9 measurement of 2026-11-01.

**Transitional presets keep the live lists.** 23 presets still read iplist.opencck.org and the itdoginfo lists per request, each on its own call:

- YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Discord — by decision: an exact recipe may miss the caches of Google and Meta inside the networks of Russian providers, and Discord voice runs over addresses outside its own network; both are open questions;
- TikTok, Viber, Notion, LinkedIn, Itch.io, Spotify, Netflix, ChatGPT, HDrezka, X (Twitter), RuTracker, Rutor, NNM-Club, Kinozal, AnimeGo, BestChange, Proton — they took opencck addresses in 3.1.9, and their catalogue recipe covered less than 90 % of the reference addresses in the build of 2026-11-01 (the reference: the addresses opencck has seen for the portal and the Moscow/Saint Petersburg resolution of antonme/ipnames, used only for this comparison);
- Telegram — by decision of 2026-11-01: its official list and AS announcements give 8 routes instead of 53 and cover 5.1 % of the reference addresses (21 of 412); its live path is that of 3.1.9, the lists plus the nine networks of `cidr.txt`, and that recipe is its reserve.

Their routes are those of 3.1.9: checked on the lists recorded on 2026-11-01 against `v3.1.9` (74 sets). RuTracker also asks for `rutracker.me` and `rutracker.nl`, the domains of `rutracker_local`; opencck gave nothing for them that day, so the check does not show what they would add. The antifilter substitute is gone: a transitional preset whose live call gives no IPv4 network takes the reserve of the catalogue, built from its recipe, and `warning` gains `Route source fallback: <keys>`. An empty answer of the lists is no longer cached for 10 minutes. Their tile price is the line count of the live lists at the build, marked «as last measured»: YouTube 621, TikTok 578, WhatsApp 513, Facebook 204, Notion 152, Instagram 133, Viber 130, LinkedIn 114, Itch.io 107, Netflix 96, Discord 93, Spotify 89, ChatGPT 56, Telegram 53, HDrezka 44, X (Twitter) 31, Proton 21, BestChange 17, RuTracker 14, Kinozal 11, AnimeGo 6, Rutor 5, NNM-Club 3.

**A new service catalogue.** Ten categories: Video, Messengers and calls, Social networks, Torrents, Films/music/anime, Games, AI, Work and development, Infrastructure, Other. The page builds them from the catalogue and names every preset in its language. New presets: Signal, Snapchat, other trackers (1337x, The Pirate Bay, Rustorka, NewStudio, BookTracker, Nyaa), other cinemas and anime (Filmix, LostFilm, Seasonvar, Kinobase, Jut.su, AniLibria, AnimeVost, Shikimori, Crunchyroll), and SoundCloud, Medium, Patreon, Grammarly, MyAnimeList in one tile. Whether each of these sites is blocked was not checked one by one.

**Twelve Russian services are taken out of the catalogue**: VK, OK, Yandex, Dzen, Rutube, Mail.ru, Wildberries, Ozon, Avito, Kinopoisk, 2GIS, MAX. They are reachable directly in Russia, and some refuse visitors who come through VPN. Their keys keep working as "retired": `/api/warp` skips them with `Presets retired: <keys>`, `/api/iplist` answers 200 with zero routes and the same warning, a settings link that names one gets a note in the routes panel. A set made only of retired presets (or of presets without data) is now `400 empty_split_tunnel` before Lab Auto and registration; in 3.1.9 `presets=vk` got 200 with the 71 antifilter networks. `rutracker_local` is an alias of `rutracker`, which takes the domains of both.

**A price on every tile, a counter by the config.** A tile shows «≈ N routes» and how exact its addresses are; a preset without data is an unavailable tile. The counter now counts `routes4`, the IPv4 lines the config will get after the cut and the merge — the number the server compares with 1000 — instead of the raw list. It cannot know two cuts made per request: the IPv4-only filter of a registration without IPv6 and the cut of a manual endpoint. Without the server the counter is the sum of the tile prices, marked as an estimate. The quick pick is now seven presets — YouTube, Telegram, Discord, Cloudflare, X (Twitter), GitHub, RuTracker — and costs at most 904 routes in this build (92 from the catalogue presets together, 812 from the live lists).

**The Endpoint never inside the routes** (new invariant I15):

- with `ipv6=1` the IPv6 routes leave out `2606:4700:d0::/48` and `2606:4700:d1::/48`, where `engage.cloudflareclient.com` answers (`2606:4700:d0::a29f:c001`); entries that do not touch them pass unchanged, IPv6 is never merged (invariant I14 now covers IPv6);
- Lab Auto skips the endpoints the split routes cover; when every fresh one is inside them, the request is refused with `503 lab_endpoints_in_routes` before registration, with the hostname button on the page, never a switch to hostname. A Lab without usable data now answers at once, before the route lists;
- the built-in endpoints of the `awg2_random` and `awg2_dpi` templates skip candidates inside the routes; the built-in pool always holds addresses in the cut WARP subnets, so a candidate always remains;
- a manual endpoint inside the routes loses its /32 (/128 with `ipv6=1`), and `warning` gains `Endpoint override inside routes: <address>`; a manual endpoint like `999.1.1.1:2408` is `400 peer_endpoint_invalid` before registration (it used to pass).

`188.114.96.0/22`, shared by WARP endpoints and ordinary sites, is still not cut: the Endpoint choice keeps the config out of it instead.

**Privacy.** Preset ids are not sent to Yandex Metrica. The server writes `[preset-stats] window=600s <preset>=<n> …` every 10 minutes: successful split generations per preset, no IP and no identifier. The privacy text now names the web server access log (IP, time, the request line with the chosen presets, referrer and browser string, kept about 15 days), these counters, and the third-party services Cloudflare, iplist.opencck.org and raw.githubusercontent.com (itdoginfo lists).

## API changes

| Where | Change |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/iplist` without presets | per preset `routes4`, `precision`, `noData`, `source`; `categories`, `aliases`, `retired`, `catalogBuiltAt`; `groupRfPopular` is the quick pick of seven |
| `GET /api/iplist?presets=…` | new `routes4`, `routes6`, `presetStatus`, `warnings`, `catalogBuiltAt`; `cidrSource` gains `catalog` and loses `antifilter`; `cidrs`, `count4`, `count6`, `count` stay the raw union of the sources, now with the IPv4 part kept and `count6` counting only IPv6 entries when `ipv6=1` |
| `/api/warp` | aliases expanded, retired keys skipped with a warning, `400 empty_split_tunnel` for a set with nothing to route (also without `routeMode`), `503 lab_endpoints_in_routes`, `400 peer_endpoint_invalid`, new `warning` lines (`Presets retired:`, `Presets without data:`, `Route source fallback:`, `Endpoint override inside routes:`), `routesTelemetrySource` gains `catalog`, `routesPresets` names canonical keys |
| `GET /api/healthcheck` | new `catalogBuiltAt` |

A catalogue snapshot that cannot be read refuses split requests with `503` and a fixed text before registration; the full tunnel never reads it.

## Evidence boundary

- The catalogue was built on 2026-11-01 from the maintainer's workstation, which goes out through a VPN client; the DoH answers are those for the three client subnets, not for any real client. Whether clients in Russia resolve the same addresses was checked for three domains only (in the design of the catalogue).
- The coverage check compares with what opencck has seen for a portal over time, without removal; outdated addresses lower the coverage, which keeps a preset on its previous source.
- No WARP device was registered for this release; whether a client loops its own packets through a route to its Endpoint was not checked on any client; the cut and the candidate filter are made without that measurement.
- The block status of the new presets and sites was not checked one by one.
