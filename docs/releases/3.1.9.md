# AmneziaWG Config Generator 3.1.9

## Split routes: four WARP endpoint subnets left out, resolved before registration, merged exactly, a warning above 1000

Prepared on 2026-11-01 after 3.1.8 (`c0ba756`). Full-tunnel configs do not change: without presets, or with `routeMode=full`, the `.conf`, `vpn://` and the response fields are those of 3.1.8.

## What changed for users

**Four WARP endpoint subnets left out.** The public route lists give the Cloudflare network `162.158.0.0/15` for Discord, X (Twitter), TikTok, OpenAI, Netflix and RuTracker (measured on 2026-11-01). It holds the subnets of the WARP endpoints: `162.159.192.1`, the first address of the built-in endpoint list and the address of `engage.cloudflareclient.com` in the measurement of 2026-11-01, and the Endpoint Lab pool, which on 2026-10-31 lay entirely in `162.159.192.0/24` (a report of another session, not re-checked here). A split config with one of these presets therefore had a route to its own `Endpoint`, in hostname mode and in Lab Auto mode, which are the defaults of the API and of the page. The IPv4 routes of presets now leave out four subnets:

```text
162.159.192.0/24, 162.159.193.0/24, 162.159.195.0/24, 162.159.204.0/24
```

The cut is the same in hostname and Lab Auto mode, so the two still differ only in `Endpoint` (invariant I11). It touches only the routes of presets, and only these four subnets: `188.114.96.0/22`, which ten presets get, holds the built-in endpoints of the `awg2_random` and `awg2_dpi` templates and may hold Lab Auto endpoints (the Lab accepts that network), and it stays routed (see the evidence boundary). A Lab Auto endpoint is outside the routes when it lies in one of the four subnets. Cloudflare sites outside these subnets stay routed; by earlier measurements of the route revision discord.com answered from `162.159.128.0/19` and several trackers from `188.114.96.3` and `188.114.97.3`, and the tests keep these addresses in the routes. Whether a client really looped its own packets through such a route was not checked, on any platform.

**An empty route list no longer costs a registration.** The routes of the selected presets are now resolved once per request, before the WARP registration. A list that comes back empty answers 502 «Сервис списков IP вернул пустой ответ…» before Cloudflare is asked; in 3.1.8 the device was registered first and the 502 came after it. All configs of `count` > 1 get the same list. The price: the list lookup no longer runs alongside the registration, so a split generation with a cold list cache waits for the lists first (the cache keeps them 10 minutes, and the page's preview usually fills it).

**`AllowedIPs` is merged exactly.** The IPv4 routes are written as the fewest aligned blocks that cover exactly the same addresses, minus the four subnets (new invariant I14). No address outside the presets' networks is added: two networks with a gap between them stay two lines. The order is the string sort of earlier versions, so a list of canonical entries (host bits zero) with nothing to merge or cut is unchanged byte for byte; an entry with host bits is written as its network (`10.0.0.1/8` → `10.0.0.0/8`), and the lists of 2026-11-01 had none. IPv6 routes (`ipv6=1`) and malformed entries pass unchanged.

On the public lists of 2026-11-01 (read from a workstation, not from the server; 25 presets got their own lists, 24 the same antifilter fallback list):

| Preset | Routes in 3.1.8 | Routes in 3.1.9 | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram | 195 | 133 | merge |
| Facebook | 270 | 204 | merge |
| Notion | 193 | 152 | merge |
| WhatsApp | 585 | 513 | merge |
| quick-select group of 26 popular presets | 2038 | 1684 | merge, still above 1000 |
| YouTube | 624 | 621 | merge |
| TikTok | 604 | 578 | the merge removes 35, the cut adds 9 |
| Discord | 87 | 93 | the merge removes 3, the cut adds 9 |
| RuTracker | 5 | 14 | the cut adds 9 |

No other preset with its own list ends up more than 6 % shorter; the presets with `162.158.0.0/15` gain up to nine lines, because that one network becomes ten blocks around the cut. `AllowedIPs` can now hold shorter prefixes and `/31` entries that the raw lists did not have; how router firmware (Keenetic, OpenWrt, Padavan) imports such lines was not checked. 24 presets, among them Google, Microsoft, GitHub and Cloudflare, got the same 71-route antifilter fallback that day, so their own lists, and whether any of them covers the endpoint subnets, were not measured.

**A warning above 1000 routes.** When `AllowedIPs` holds more than 1000 IPv4 routes, the config is still given and `warning` gains one line, which the page shows in its language with the three numbers:

```text
Route budget: 1009 IPv4 routes in AllowedIPs (1000 in the source lists) is above the limit of 1000; phones and routers may handle a list this long unstably.
```

There is no refusal. The limit stays 1000; no client threshold has been measured.

**The counter on the page.** At exactly 1000 IPv4 CIDR the counter is now yellow, not red, and the preview no longer says the limit is exceeded; red starts at 1001. Unchecked tiles are still disabled from 1000. The settings text says that adjacent subnets are merged and names the four cut subnets.

## The API

| Request | 3.1.8 | 3.1.9 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| split, a route list that comes back empty | registration, then 502 | 502 before registration, no request to Cloudflare |
| split, every route inside the four subnets | registration, config with these routes | 502 before registration |
| split, routes covering `162.159.192.0/24` | the route in `AllowedIPs` | left out |
| split, more than 1000 IPv4 routes in `AllowedIPs` | config, no warning | config, `warning` `Route budget: …` |
| split, `count` = 3 | the lists looked up for every config (the cache answered the second and third) | one lookup |
| full tunnel, `/api/iplist`, refusals before registration, Cloudflare failures | — | unchanged |

`/api/iplist` does not change: `count4` and `cidrs` are the raw list, and the page counter counts them. The server compares the list after the cut and the merge with 1000. The two numbers can differ in both directions: a set the counter shows just under 1000 can get the warning (the cut adds lines), and a set the counter shows above 1000 can stay below it on the server (the merge removes lines). Fields that report the route numbers are planned for a minor release.

The `Route budget:` line is the only new text in `warning`; the other lines and the order of `warning` are those of 3.1.8.

## Evidence boundary

- Aggregation is checked against an independent oracle (`__tests__/helpers/cidr-coverage.js` shares no code with the module): exact coverage on 1100 distinct seeded random lists, the minimal number of blocks against a prefix-tree count, every edge case of the spec one test each, the subtraction around holes in the middle, side by side and at the edges, and the three largest recorded lists (`__tests__/cidr-aggregate.test.js`, `__tests__/invariant-i14-route-aggregation.test.js`).
- Through the real handler with a fake network: every one of the 49 presets gives exactly the preset's addresses minus the four subnets, discord.com and `188.114.96.3` stay; Lab Auto and hostname give the same `AllowedIPs`; the full tunnel and the IPv6 part are unchanged; `vpn://` carries the same `AllowedIPs`; `mobile=1`, `router=1` and the 3.1.8 IPv4-only degradation hold (`__tests__/route-aggregation-api.test.js`); the routes are resolved once and before registration, and a 502 by routes is not counted as a registration outcome (`__tests__/warp-route-preresolve.test.js`).
- Against `v3.1.8` with the same fake network: 48 split sets (four modes; `telegram`, `telegram,control4`, `discord`, the three largest presets, `count=3`, `mobile=1`, `ipv6=1`, `router=1`, Lab Auto) differ only in `AllowedIPs`, whose IPv4 part equals the one of 3.1.8 minus the four subnets and whose IPv6 part is equal; 68 full-tunnel sets are equal.
- The page: the counter at 999, 1000 and 1001 and the localised warning on `/` and `/en` in a real browser, on the source and on the minified build (`e2e/route-limit.e2e.js`).
- Not checked: whether any client looped its packets through a route to its own endpoint; how router firmware handles the merged prefixes; the IPv6 endpoint prefixes with `ipv6=1`; `188.114.96.0/22`, which the lists give for ten presets and which still covers the built-in endpoints of the `awg2_random` and `awg2_dpi` templates and any Lab Auto endpoint the Lab verifies there — it is shared with ordinary sites and is left to a later patch that chooses the `Endpoint` outside the routes. No WARP registration was made for this release.

## For developers

- `src/server/cidrAggregate.js` (new): `aggregateCidrs(list, { exclude })` → `{ cidrs, before4, after4, count6, invalid4 }`. Intervals on `Number`, merge of overlapping, nested and adjacent intervals, subtraction of `exclude`, greedy split into aligned blocks, string sort.
- `src/server/routeBudget.js` (new): `ROUTE_LIMIT = 1000`, `WARN_RATIO = 0.8`, `classifyBudget` (`over` only above the limit), `routeBudgetWarning`.
- `src/server/warpEndpointPrefixes.js` (new): `WARP_ENDPOINT_PREFIXES_V4`.
- `api/warp.js`: the handler resolves the routes after the Lab selection and before `generateMultipleWarpConfigs` and passes them down as `routeOpts.resolvedRoutes`; `generateWarpConfig` keeps the concurrent lookup only for a call without them. The IPv4-only filter of a registration without IPv6 stays per config. `resolveAllowedIpsFromPresets` cuts and merges, then checks for an empty list.
- Frontend: `settings.js` comparisons (`warn` up to the limit, `over` above it, tiles from the limit), `routes_over_budget` in `KNOWN_API_WARNINGS`, `warningText` with the three numbers, locale keys `risk_routes_over_budget` and `routing_allowedips_limit`.
- Tests: new `cidr-aggregate`, `route-budget`, `invariant-i14-route-aggregation`, `route-aggregation-api`, `warp-route-preresolve`, `route-limit-ui`, `e2e/route-limit`; fixtures `__tests__/fixtures/route-lists/`. Changed expectations: `routing-frontend-guards.test.js` (`<=` and `>` per line), and three tests of `unhandled-rejection-audit.test.js` — a split request with failing lists and a refusing Cloudflare is now the routes' 502 before registration, every list down is 502 with no request to Cloudflare, and the offline `server.js` answers 502 instead of 503 to `routeMode=split&presets=discord` (discord has no static IPv4 routes). Their assertions about unhandled rejections, the live process and timers are unchanged.
