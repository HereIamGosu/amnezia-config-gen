## Design audit

impeccable 4.1.0 (`npx impeccable detect --json public/`, engine 0.1.11) on 3.0.2: 166 findings; on this release: 154. `low-contrast` 4 → 0 and `undersized-ui-text` 8 → 0. The remaining findings are the 3.0 visual language (Inter, glows, gradients, hairline borders with wide shadows), Russian typography (em dashes) and false positives (1–2 px stars of the hero starfield reported as radial halos); they are not defects and were left as they are.
